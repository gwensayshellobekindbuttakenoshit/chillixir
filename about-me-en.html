Hi, I'm Gwen

I know, first hand, what it's like to live in the spectrum of every day stress and anxiety, anywhere from living paycheck to paycheck (not knowing what the next meal is going to be, not having enough for rent, having to pick between paying for the phone bill and a meal, etc.) to now having to adapt to a new country, a new culture, an old languange I haven't even thought of in 25 years, finding a new career, among many other things.

Way before leaving everything I know in the U.S. back in December 2024, I started making magnesium sprays for fun. I loved the idea of "healing" people "one spray at a time". It was a cute little hobby I thought I started. Then, after arriving to Mexico and experiencing the bureaucracy of a country and culture I'm not a part of and needing desperately to find some sort of calm amidst the chaos, I was reminded of magnesium. It is better absorbed by the body topically (applied directly to the skin) than orally (as in pills. Please do not ingest Chillixir products lol) and it acts as a natural muscle relaxant, helping reduce cramps, soreness, even migraines; it supports sleep quality, and even more.

I wanted to find an organic product, without chemicals and that was also made by someone who wouldn't vote against people who aren't like them-anywhere in the world. And THAT reminded me of that "cute little hobby" I had way back when and that's how Chillixir was born. Made by LGBTQIA+ immigrants.

Not everyone enjoys sprays, so I incorporated creams, and not everyone enjoys sprays nor creams (or prefers something with an easier and quick application), so that's how the roll-ons come into play.

The name Chillixir came from the feeling you get when you finally chill and the tension melts away, and the elixir that helps you get there. One little word, big hopes.

Every bottle of Chillixir is handmade in Mexico by me with intention, high frequency environment, 100% natural ingredients, cruelty-free, and in amber glass containers to reduce plastic and protect the contents inside.

Every scent offered is chosen not just for how it smells, but for how it supports the body, soothes the mind and stirs something deeper. Because wellness isn't just physical, it's spiritual, too.

Lavender- it's primarily associated with the Crown and Third Eye Chakras, helping to enhance intuition, spirtual awareness, and clarity. It's also known for promoting deep relaxation, reducing stress, anxiety, quieting the mind, improving sleep quality, calming the nervous system, purifying spaces, warding off negative energy, promoting inner peace, and facilitating meditation and prayer.

Bergamot- it is known for simultaneously uplifting mood and calming the nervous system, reducing anxiety, stress and fatigue. It's also known for improving sleep quality. It's believed to clear negative energy, heal emotional wounds like grief and anxiety, open the Heart Chakra, and stimulate the Third Eye Chakra.

Limited edition for the Spring time, Ylang Ylang- it is known for reducing stress, anxiety, and anger while promoting relaxation and improving sleep. It promotes emotional balance, open the Heart Chakra, deepen meditation, purify the aura, clear negative energy, and ward off evil spirits or nightmares. Its sweet and floral aroma is believed to calm the mind, and foster a sense of inner peace and self-love.

Limited edition for the Fall, Frankincense- it is known to reduce cortisol, and enhance mental focus, promote grounding, clarity, and divine connection, dispel negative energies, purify sacred spaces, and open the Third Eye and Crown Chakras. Its calming aroma is believed to alleviate stress, anxiety, grief, and nervousness while promoting inner peace, emotional balance, and resilience.



Choosing Chillixir isn't just buying a spray, cream or roll-on. It's supporting LGBTQIA+ and immigrant communities. My goal is to, one day in a near future, be able to get Chillixir profits to donate to those in need.
