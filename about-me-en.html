We don't just sell products, we build community

Chillixir was born from a personal need: finding calm amidst chaos. As an LGBTQIA+ refugee, I've experienced firsthand how stress and anxiety build up in the body. Seeking relief, I was reminded of the power of magnesium and lavender, wisdom that has been around for generations. But when I looked for products, I only found generic options, full of chemicals and without soul.

I decided we deserved something better. Something natural, effective, and made with intention. So I created it myself.

The name Chillixir came from exactly that feeling- the moment you finally chill, like the tension melts away, and the elixir that helps get you there. It's a little word made from big hopes.

Today, every bottle of Chillixir is handmade in Mexico with 100% natural ingredients, cruelty-free, and in glass containers to reduce plastic. But this goes beyond ingredients: it's a commitment to holistic wellness, inclusion, and sustainability.

Choosing Chillixir isn't just buying a spray or cream. It's supporting a dream driven by resilience and the belief that we all deserve deep, accessible peace.

Every scent offered — from lavender to bergamot, frankincense to ylang ylang — is chosen not just for how it smells, but for how it supports the body, soothes the mind, and stirs something deeper. Because wellness isn't just physical. It's spiritual too.

Thank you for being here and being part of this.
