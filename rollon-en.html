What is the Magnesium Roll-on?

A concentrated blend of magnesium and essential oil of your choosing (or without) in a convenient roll-on bottle. Designed for targeted application to temples, wrists, and pulse points, it's your on-the-go ritual for stress relief and grounding.

Why You'll Love It

  • Targeted relief exactly where you need it
  • Perfect for bags or pockets
  • Fast-absorbing and non-greasy
  • Pure magnesium + essential oils for instant calm
  • Ideal for stress, tension headaches, or moments of overwhelm

How to use

Apply to temples, wrists, or behind the ears. Gently roll and massage in. Use anytime you need to reset — before a meeting, during travel, or as part of your bedtime ritual.
